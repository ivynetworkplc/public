2 Protection of Ivy Network PLC Brand Identity

The Ivy Network PLC name, logo and visual identity represent the Company’s official brand. They identify our technology services, programmes and digital products to the public.

Unauthorised use includes

  • Creating fake accounts that impersonate Ivy Network PLC
  • Impersonating the Company or its programmes in communications or online platforms
  • Using the logo or brand marks without permission
  • Creating misleading services, websites or materials that use the brand name
  • Suggesting endorsement or affiliation where none exists

Ivy Network PLC reserves the right to take appropriate action against unauthorised usage of its name, logo and brand identity. This may include requests for removal, formal notices and, where necessary, legal remedies available under applicable law.

Brand enquiries: For legitimate partnership, media or licensing requests involving the Ivy Network PLC brand, contact team@ivynetwork.co.uk.

3 Digital Rights Management

Ivy Network PLC supports protection of digital rights for businesses, organisations and content creators. Digital rights protection in this context includes awareness, guidance and practical support related to:

  • Content ownership protection
  • Copyright monitoring and awareness
  • Unauthorised usage reporting
  • Digital content protection practices
  • Brand protection considerations
  • Intellectual property awareness education

As a technology company focused on digital solutions and Digital Rights Management initiatives, Ivy Network PLC aims to help rights holders understand and assert their interests in an increasingly digital environment.

Specific services or tools related to digital rights may evolve over time. Product-specific terms will apply where dedicated platforms or applications are introduced.

4 Supporting Content Creators

Ivy Network PLC believes creators should have meaningful control over their original digital work. Content creators, digital publishers and online brands may seek guidance regarding:

6 Copyright Complaint & Content Removal Requests

If a copyright owner or authorised representative believes that content associated with Ivy Network PLC websites, platforms or services violates their rights, they may submit a complaint to the Company.

Ivy Network PLC may review complaints and take appropriate action where warranted, which may include:

  • Removing or restricting access to disputed content
  • Requesting corrections or clarifications
  • Contacting involved parties for further information
  • Referring the matter for further review where required

Actions depend on verification of ownership claims and the information available. Incomplete or unverified complaints may not result in immediate action.

Reporting process

1

Submit complaint

Send a clear copyright complaint to the Copyright Department.

2

Provide evidence

Include ownership evidence and details of the disputed material.

3

Review

Ivy Network PLC reviews the request and supporting information.

4

Action

Appropriate action is taken based on verification and findings.

Submit complaints to: copyrights@ivynetwork.co.uk
Include your contact details, a description of the work you own, the location of the allegedly infringing material, and a statement that you are authorised to act on behalf of the rights holder.
