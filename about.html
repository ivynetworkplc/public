Company Profile

Who We Are

Ivy Network PLC is a technology company providing digital solutions, software development, IT services and technology programmes. We create solutions that help organisations improve efficiency, embrace innovation and participate in the digital economy.

Our work spans software products, websites, mobile applications, consulting, cloud and cybersecurity guidance, and community-focused technology education through initiatives such as Elite Tour.

Part of Tech Insiders Limited — established 2019
Our Story

From Vision to Digital Solutions

Ivy Network PLC is part of Tech Insiders Limited, an organisation established with a vision of using technology to solve real-world challenges. From that foundation, the Company has expanded its focus into software development, digital services and technology empowerment.

Through innovation, practical delivery and a commitment to reliable systems, Ivy Network PLC continues to support digital transformation and technology adoption for businesses, organisations and communities.
