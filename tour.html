About the Programme

What is Elite Tour?

Elite Tour is an initiative by Ivy Network PLC designed to bring technology education and digital skills closer to communities across Tanzania and East Africa. It focuses on digital skills development, technology awareness, innovation, youth empowerment, and community learning.
