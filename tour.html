About Elite Tour

Elite Tour is an initiative by Ivy Network PLC designed to empower young people through technology education, digital skills development and innovation awareness.


Through Elite Tour, Ivy Network PLC brings technology learning opportunities closer to communities across Tanzania and East Africa.
