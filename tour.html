About the Programme

What is Elite Tour?

Elite Tour is an initiative by Ivy Network PLC designed to bring technology education and digital skills closer to communities across Tanzania and East Africa.

The programme focuses on digital skills development, technology awareness, innovation, youth empowerment, and community learning — equipping participants with practical knowledge for the digital economy.

Important: Elite Tour is a technology training tour, not a tourism event. Participants engage in structured digital skills sessions and technology awareness activities.

Digital Skills

Practical training in essential digital competencies for the modern workforce.

Innovation

Encouraging creative problem-solving and technology-driven solutions.

Youth Empowerment

Building capacity among young people for digital participation and opportunity.

Community Learning

Bringing technology education directly to local communities across regions.
